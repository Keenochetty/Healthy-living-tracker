# Health Safety Wording Checklist

## Blocked Wording
Do not use these phrases in user-facing health guidance:
- safe day
- unsafe day
- you are fertile
- you are pregnant
- contraception failed
- food cancelled contraception
- you must take
- stop taking
- your baby is delayed
- normal growth
- abnormal growth
- give this dose
- your baby is sick
- ignore this symptom
- danger
- failed
- cured
- diagnosed

## Preferred Wording
- Could not save. Try again.
- Review this with a healthcare professional.
- This may need professional confirmation.
- Based on your logs.
- This is for tracking only.
- Consider confirming with a healthcare professional or clinic.
- Follow the label or healthcare professional’s instructions.

## Realm-Specific Rules
- Women’s Health: fertile windows, ovulation, and period predictions are estimates.
- Pregnancy: activation and positive test notes never diagnose pregnancy.
- Baby / Child: growth, milestones, symptoms, and solids are tracking-only.
- Medication/Supplements: no dose advice, start/stop guidance, or safe/unsafe combinations.
- Biometrics: no normal/abnormal labels; blood pressure/glucose mention professional guidance.
- Records: lab results are organized only and should be reviewed with a professional.
- AI: all health actions are drafts and require user confirmation before saving.
