# HealthOS Supplement Caution Model

Date: 2026-06-17

## Principle

Supplements are not automatically safe because they are natural or over the counter.

## Allowed Copy

Use review-prompt language:

- "Review supplement use with a healthcare professional."
- "Supplement-medication caution needs review."
- "Interaction checking is not connected yet."

## Disallowed Copy

Do not claim:

- supplement benefits without sourced Trusted Content,
- supplement safety,
- interaction detection,
- clinical suitability,
- dosage recommendations.

## Professional Review

Pregnancy, child, allergy, medication-combination, and complex health-condition contexts should use professional-review recommendation copy.
