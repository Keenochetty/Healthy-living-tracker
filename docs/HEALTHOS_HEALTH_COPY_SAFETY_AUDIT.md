# HealthOS Health Copy Safety Audit

Date: 2026-06-18

## Search Terms Reviewed

Reviewed active source for diagnosis, cure, guarantee, unsafe medication, pregnancy, allergy, emergency, and baby-development wording.

## Findings

- Most `diagnose` hits are disclaimers saying the app does not diagnose.
- Medication, pregnancy, baby/child, women’s health, and trusted-content copy generally uses education/review language rather than diagnosis or prescription language.
- `interaction detected`, `take this dose`, `stop taking`, `start taking`, and similar unsafe directive claims were not found as active recommendations in the inspected paths.

## Fixes

- No unsafe medical advice copy required replacement in this pass.
- Existing safety copy remains: AI can make mistakes, review with professionals, and urgent symptoms require medical help.

## Remaining Work

- Legal and clinical copy should still receive human review before store submission.
