# HealthOS Style Sheet 12 - Pregnancy Realm

## Purpose

Pregnancy is a private-first HealthOS realm for pregnancy planning, due-date progress, week and trimester context, practical checklists, family update foundations, care team access foundations, trusted-source education, and safe AI question preparation.

## Rules

- Pregnancy is private by default.
- Pregnancy must not be added to the visible bottom nav.
- Do not invent due dates, week numbers, trimesters, baby growth facts, symptoms, appointments, supplements, family updates, care team access, or article links.
- Do not create pregnancy state without user confirmation.
- Do not create baby profiles, sharing permissions, care team invites, reminders, or records without routing to existing flows or confirmed handlers.
- Do not diagnose symptoms or recommend medication/supplement changes.

## Required Sections

- Pregnancy header and privacy chip
- Setup/transformation foundation
- Due-date progress hero
- Week and trimester overview
- Weekly baby growth source-ready card
- Quick actions
- Quick log sheet
- Mother health summary
- Appointments and reminders
- Supplements and medication connection
- Mom checklist
- Partner checklist
- Hospital bag checklist
- After-birth plan
- Baby profile creation foundation
- Family updates foundation
- Care team access foundation
- Trusted-source articles
- AI pregnancy question card
- Calendar/privacy/records connector
