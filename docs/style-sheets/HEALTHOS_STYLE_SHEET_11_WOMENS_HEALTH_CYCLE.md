# HealthOS Style Sheet 11 - Women's Health / Cycle Realm

## Purpose

This sheet defines the private-first HealthOS Women's Health / Cycle realm.

## Scope

- Redesign the active Cycle route only.
- Use HealthOS shell, tokens, cards, pills, and chart primitives.
- Keep Women's Health/Cycle out of the visible bottom nav.
- Use existing local cycle/women's health storage where safe.
- Do not fake cycle predictions, fertile windows, contraception data, sex logs, mood/symptom logs, pregnancy state, or article links.
- Do not expose private data to family.
- Do not start pregnancy mode or change sharing without explicit supported flow.

## Required Sections

- Private header and privacy chip
- Cycle overview hero
- Compact cycle visual
- Quick log actions and sheet
- Pattern chart cards
- Mood/symptom section
- Sex-day logging foundation
- Contraception section
- FAQ accordion
- Source preview
- AI Q&A card
- Pregnancy transition card
- Calendar/privacy card

## Safety

Cycle and contraception estimates are informational only. The app must not diagnose symptoms, guarantee fertility or contraception outcomes, or replace professional advice.
