# HealthOS Trusted Content Copyright And AI Rules

## Allowed

- Title
- Short summary
- Source name
- Source URL
- Image URL
- Author
- Publisher
- Published/reviewed/retrieved dates
- Tags
- Category
- Realm targeting
- Source quality labels
- Safety disclaimers

## Not Allowed

- Full copied external articles
- Paywalled article text
- Scraped article bodies
- AI-generated articles presented as official content
- Invented citations
- Unlabeled AI summaries

## AI Rules

AI summaries must preserve the source link, use `ai_summary_status`, and show a warning unless reviewed. AI-generated content is not trusted medical content by default.
