# HealthOS Privacy + Legal Readiness

This is a readiness checklist, not final legal text.

## Privacy Policy Topics Needed

- Account identity data.
- Health records and document storage.
- Medication and supplement reminders.
- Pregnancy, cycle, baby/child, and family/caregiver data.
- AI processing and review-first imports.
- Trusted content and source links.
- Device permissions: camera, photos, notifications, biometrics, documents.
- Data export and deletion.
- Retention and account deletion.
- Family sharing and explicit permissions.
- Children/child profile privacy.
- Notification lock-screen privacy.

## Terms Topics Needed

- No diagnosis or treatment claims.
- User review required before saving/importing AI or scan output.
- User responsibility for medical decisions.
- Third-party services and source links.
- Subscription/payment terms later if billing is added.
- Acceptable use and emergency guidance.

## Medical Disclaimer Topics Needed

- HealthOS organizes information and does not replace professional care.
- AI can make mistakes.
- Medication, pregnancy, baby/child, fertility, cycle, fitness, nutrition, and lab/record content require professional review when needed.
- Emergency symptoms require urgent care, not app guidance.

## AI Disclosure Topics

- OpenAI/API calls must remain backend-only.
- The mobile app must not contain OpenAI API keys.
- AI outputs must be review-first.
- AI outputs must not auto-save or auto-share.
- User-provided health text/images may be processed by backend AI only when the user takes an explicit action.

## Records / Storage Topics

- Private storage model.
- Signed URL expiry.
- File access permissions.
- No raw storage paths in UI.
- Upload failure and deletion behavior.

## Account/Data Topics

- Account deletion.
- Data deletion.
- Data export.
- Profile/contact update errors.
- Family member/caregiver permission revocation.

## Later Professional Review

Final privacy policy, terms, medical disclaimer, child/family data language, and store disclosures must be reviewed by qualified legal/compliance/professional advisors before release.
